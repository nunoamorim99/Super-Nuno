# Credits

**Super Nuno** — an autobiographical 2D platformer by Nuno Amorim.

## Art & Audio
- **[Kenney](https://kenney.nl)** — all game art is based on Kenney's CC0 asset packs,
  edited for this project:
  - [Pixel Platformer](https://kenney.nl/assets/pixel-platformer) (tiles, characters, backgrounds)
  - Pixel Platformer Blocks (block variants)
  - Kenney CC0 audio packs (sound effects / music)

  Kenney assets are released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)** — public domain.
  Attribution is not required; it is given here with gratitude. Support Kenney at
  [kenney.nl/donate](https://kenney.nl/donate).
